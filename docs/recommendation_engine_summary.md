# Recommendation Engine Summary

## Objective

The objective of this phase was to convert churn predictions into actionable business recommendations that support customer retention.

## Methodology

The recommendation engine combines churn probability, customer risk category, customer attributes, and cluster assignments to generate rule-based retention strategies.

Business rules were designed to simulate real-world customer retention policies, ensuring that different customer profiles receive appropriate recommendations.

## Results

Each customer received a personalized recommendation based on their predicted churn risk and customer profile.

A recommendation summary was also generated to identify the most common retention strategies required across the customer base.

## Business Value

The recommendation engine enables organizations to move beyond prediction by providing actionable next steps. This helps customer service, marketing, and retention teams prioritize interventions, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce churn through targeted campaigns.
