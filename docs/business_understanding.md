# Customer Churn Intelligence Platform

## Business Problem

Customers are leaving the company, resulting in revenue loss.

## Objective

Predict churn before it happens and provide actionable recommendations.

## Stakeholders

- Customer Success
- Marketing
- Sales
- Leadership

## Success Metrics

### Business

- Churn Rate
- Revenue At Risk

### Machine Learning

- Recall
- F1 Score
- ROC-AUC

## Expected Outcome

Reduce customer churn through early intervention.
